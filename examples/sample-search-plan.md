# Sample search plan

Fictional candidate: customer support and onboarding experience, HubSpot, Excel reporting, and team scheduling. Target: Toronto hybrid or remote within Canada, full-time, CAD 65,000+ base salary.

These are suggested queries, not evidence of current vacancies. Execute them through an authorized search integration, then verify employer originals through permitted routes. Preserve only result data the provider permits retaining. Do not automate LinkedIn pages or consumer search-result pages; see the [source access policy](../references/job-sources.md). Record actual discovery and verification separately.

| Group | Keywords or roles | Why they fit | Example query |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Close roles | Customer success specialist; client success associate; customer onboarding specialist | Direct connection to onboarding and customer support | `("customer success specialist" OR "client success associate") Toronto hybrid` |
| Close functions | Client onboarding; account support; customer operations | Finds equivalent work under different titles | `"client onboarding" "Canada" jobs` |
| Adjacent roles | Operations coordinator; service coordinator; client operations associate | Transfers scheduling, reporting, and handoff experience | `("operations coordinator" OR "service coordinator") Toronto Excel` |
| Adjacent domain | Implementation coordinator; software support specialist | Builds on software customer experience | `"implementation coordinator" "remote" "Canada"` |
| Stretch roles | Customer success manager; implementation specialist | May require independent portfolios or technical implementation experience | `"customer success manager" "Canada" HubSpot` |
| Tools and evidence | HubSpot; CRM; Excel; onboarding; reporting | Adds specific demonstrated competencies | `"customer operations" HubSpot Excel jobs Canada` |
| Work arrangement | Hybrid Toronto; remote Canada; Ontario | Keeps location eligibility visible | `"customer success" "remote Canada" careers` |
| Employment type | Full-time; permanent | Matches the requested employment type | `"client services" Toronto "full-time"` |
| Employer sources | Employer career pages and original applicant-tracking-system posts | Helps locate original descriptions | `"customer onboarding" "Canada" site:jobs.lever.co` |
| Optional French expansion | Réussite client; succès client; coordonnateur des opérations | Useful only if the user wants French-language or bilingual openings | `"coordonnateur des opérations" Toronto emploi` |

Keep Toronto/Canada eligibility and the compensation floor as hard constraints. If results are sparse, broaden equivalent titles or industries first; do not silently include US-only remote work or raise seniority. Salary omissions require clarification. Search syntax varies by provider, so simplify queries when operators are unsupported.
