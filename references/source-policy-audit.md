# Source policy audit

Checked **2026-09-10, America/New_York**, using official policy and documentation pages. This document preserves policy evidence for maintainers; it is not a resource directory. The skill's current source exclusions can be stricter than a provider's published permissions. This is policy research, not legal certification or a promise that every future use complies. Some official terms were unreadable or have unresolved scope.

## How to read the decisions

- **Excluded:** outside the current skill's active sources. Do not search, fetch, recommend its jobs or homepage, or list it as a manual fallback in a report. Mention it only when explaining an exclusion or in this maintenance audit. Do not ingest its output through another service. Published personal-use or conditional API/feed permission does not change this decision.
- **Conditional candidate:** retained in the active strategy, but usable only when the actual license, user/account scope, purpose, attribution, linking, rate, and retention conditions cover the search and report. A documented route's existence does not mean an integration was installed or implemented.
- **Unverified:** an applicable permission or current policy could not be established. The relevant route stays off; continue through other permitted evidence.

Current candidates are employer sources with established permission, conditional Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby public listing APIs, the conditional Remote OK feed, and an existing authorized USAJOBS API. O*NET and ESCO are separate terminology resources. All other job boards and vacancy services below are excluded; unauthorized Workday/JazzHR/iCIMS/Avature tenant routes are excluded as well. Adding an excluded source requires a maintainer policy revision and a verified applicable grant. The agent must not enable it during a search because it discovers an API, connector, feed, or potential exception.

Check a provider and access method during setup, record the policy URL/date and granted scope, and reuse that determination until terms, scope, account, or access conditions change. Do not ask the user for permission before every job. A user's consent or existing login cannot supply missing platform rights. Employer and tenant-specific terms still need to fit the actual route; this review did not verify every future employer, private contract, or subscription.

Search-provider rights and destination-content rights are separate. Do not use targeted indexed searches, caches, mirrors, pasted restricted output, or scraper vendors to work around an exclusion or absent grant. A public-search-engine indexing exception does not grant this skill downstream copying or AI-processing rights. Preserve required attribution/application links for active licensed feed results. An independently found employer original can use its own evidence and terms. Keep no restricted snippets or raw responses in reports, JSON, or checkpoints.

**Existing host search tools:** use only their documented supported functionality and established use/retention scope. Do not infer which engine or private agreement a host uses from returned URLs, or claim this audit certifies that contract. Do not add a raw search-page scraper or silently assume one provider's API terms apply to another integration. If a route is known to forbid the host's transcript/report retention, skip it before collection; deleting a later report does not undo upstream storage. If no compatible live route is available, provide a keyword plan and explain that current vacancies were not verified. Do not fill the gap with excluded-source recommendations.

Do not target excluded boards through another engine. For incidental results from excluded sources, do not open, quote, reconstruct, score, save, or recommend their listings or homepages; continue with independently obtained, permitted employer evidence. Use source-exclusion controls when supported to reduce these results. Search and employer evidence remain distinct in the coverage ledger. An exclusion may be explained in that ledger without turning it into a resource recommendation.

## Practical limits

Retain the 20-company target, up to three recommended jobs per company, and full matching/company tables. Use other permitted sources when a route is off and report evidence gaps honestly. Do not invent vacancies or guarantee identical coverage.

The built-in collector only implements public listing GETs for Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby. Its host/path checks do not verify contracts or content rights, and the skill is not a firewall controlling every host tool. No new conditional integration, provider account, permission request, purchase, or job application was performed by this audit.
